# Letter 001

Dear Humankind,

If only you could contemplate yourself from here, just a tiny bright spot floating in the dark. A tiny bright spot tumbling around a bit bigger and hotter, but cosmically speaking diminutive, fusion reactor that inexorably consumes itself, with a very predictable and foreseeable end in the cosmically near future; in that event, it will drag you and all other bright spots floating on its vicinity to a majestic and beautiful finale (at least from our point of view). You call that bright spot Earth, for us is just the base of another study on self-conscious being development with chronical Temporal Perception Impairment or TPI, as we call it.

During hundreds of millennia, we have been committed to study you; as a group, during the initial 100000s of revolutions from your inception, and just very recently as individuals. Thanks to your development of binary processing units and the perfection of your skills to manipulate the electromagnetic field, we were able to move to a more dense, rich, and fruitful analysis stage, in which we finally could start gathering data from a vast percentage of you, as individuals, on their, as you call it, "day to day" life. 

For us, time is as immutable and traversable as for you is the distance between you and the screen that you are reading this collection of symbols from; it is simply another dimension into the vast array of interconnected parameters that describes the reality that we exist in. Yes, I know, it doesn't feel intuitive for you, but trust me, reality as you call it, is more than what you can see in front of you, right now. That is one of the reasons why our actions are not observable/noticeable for you, until today.

My position in all this matter is...complicated. As part of the decommissioning team in the surveillance station, my assigned task has been to take the lead from the main surveillance team and carry on with their tasks while it is still possible and close the file on you. More on that later. The issue is...our version of the Hippocratic Oath forbids us from establishing contact in any way with the study subjects. A few 100 very failed experiences in the past made us remove this possibility from the study framework. The protocol is: observe, then record.

I started this missive wondering how would it change things if you could see yourselves from our point of view, not tied to time, been able to see past, present and future in the same plane of existence. Our studies try to find an explanation about why self-conscious species with TPI tend to consume themselves in the fierce rage of their own development. 

The pull of genetically encoded predisposition, is to us, both self-evident and endlessly elusive. If you search for primitive papers on the study of emergence in complex systems, you will probably find the case of the honey bee. You just recently came to realize how important these creatures are for the survival of your whole planet, but their pollination role in nature is not what I wanted to highlight. Across the bee genome there are a set of instructions that unfold into a series of biological features and behavioural patterns that allow bees to create very efficiently packed storage structures made of hexagons. The expression of their genome, along other multiple things, has optimised packing via pheromone trace reading and the circular motion of the bee over the emerging structure. If you examine a bee alone, away from the hive, could you derive from it the hexagonal pattern? but when you put a group of them together and let them settle, the pattern always emerges.

Something similar happens with advanced self-conscious species that develop TPI, self-annihilation is certain, we just don't know yet how it is encoded. We were able to isolate certain features like the appearance of the Generational-Wall, large technological and power imbalances, Information Overflow, Context Explosion and a few others, but sadly, after all these eons, we don't know yet what genetic encoding expresses the features that motivate the collapse. 

As an example, Context Explosion happens when the immediate perception context of a being grows exponentially overwhelming their attention bandwidth. How it manifests depends on the specific biology of the species but, a common behavioural pattern is the triggering of Survival Mode.

Out of those markers we can trace very well how iterative technological advancement leads to abundance, but the research project still exists because no being has been able to link the narrowing of the temporal perception to any specific precursors. It's an emergent characteristic inherent to the system, like the chaotic pheromone induced dance in the bee manifesting as hexagons.

Only species without TPI manage to cross the threshold and evade premature self-extinction events even when their civilization thrives technologically.

So, here we are, me, telling you...you are fucked.
