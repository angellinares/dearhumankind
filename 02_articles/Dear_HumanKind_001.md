Dear Humankind,

If only you could contemplate yourself from here, just a tiny bright spot floating in the dark. A tyny bright spot tumbling around a bit bigger and hotter, but cosmically speaking diminute, fusion reactor that inexorably consumes itself (yep, "your" Sun), with a very predictible and forseable end in the (comsmically) near future; in that event, it will drag you and all other bright spots floating on its vecinity to a magestic and beautiful finalle (at least from our point of view). You call that bright spot Earth, for us is just the base of another study on selfconcious being development with chronical Temporal Perception Impairment or TPI, as we call it.

During hundreds of millenias (as you call the specific quantity of time that takes your planet to orbit a 1000 times ÿour" Sun) we have been commited to study you; as a group during the initial 100000s of revolutions from your inception, and just very recently as individuals. Thanks to your development of binary processing units and the perfection of your skills to manipulate the electromanetic field, we were able to move to a more dense, rich and fruitfull analysis stage, in which we finally could start gathering data from a bast percentage of you, as individuals, on their, as you call it, "day to day" life. 

For us, time is as unmutable and traversable as for you is the distance between you and the screen that you are reading this collection of symbols from; it is simply another dimension into the bast array of interconnected parameters that describes the reality that we exist in. Yes, I know, it doesn't feel intuitive for you, but trust me, reality as you call it, is more than what you can see in front of you, right now. That's one of the reasons why our actions are not observable/noticeable for you, until today.

My position in all this matter is...complicated. As part of the decomisioning team in the surveillance station, my assigned task has been to take the lead from the main survelliance team and carry on with their tasks while it is still possible and close the file on you. More on that later. The issue is...our verion of the Hyppocratic Oath forbid us from stablishing contact in any way with the study subjects. A few 100 very failed experiences in the past made us remove this possibility from the study framework. The protocol is: observe, then record.

I started this missive wondering how would it change things if you could see yourselves from our point of view, not tied to time, been able to see past, present and future in the same plane of existance. Our studies try to find an explanation about why self-concious species with TPI tend to consume themselves in the fierce rage of their own development. As some beings are encoded genetically to perform relatively complex tasks, like your "bees" producing hexagonal patterns in their hives product of simple actions triggered by pheromone traces, our studies seem to confirm that is inevitable that self-contious species suffering TPI consume themselves in their evolutionary path without understanding how, their learned experiences (past, history) could bring answers to their (present?) problems and provide the key to avoid extinction. It seems that, as we call it, the Generational Wall is too tall for you to look over. Recently, that generational border has squished your temporal perception to an even narrower segment of your existence as specie, sadly and paradogically now, when you have better access than ever to the information and foundations of your own civilization from the past. In the same way that the actions of a short-sighted human, when is not wearing glasses, feel helpless and create anxiaty in other humans with perfect sight, your recent turning into self-anhiilation trigers on me hard to process feelings and dilemas. 

I wonder if our aseptic research approach driven by learnings from other similar observations, could be making us to miss an opportunity for starting a more pragmatic approach in energy preservation terms as could be an Endangered Species Protection Program. Once contiousness rises, natural selection (as we prefer to call it "cosmic entropy redistribution inertia") becomes a tricky affair: should the researcher in the savanna interfere with the course of nature when a gacelle is accidentally injure after falling from a slippery rock surprised by a thunder stricking close? Is it even the same case? The gacelle didn't know about the lightning and his response would be merely instinctive under certain sound stimuli. In your case, you could not be more informed about the matter.

When the research project started, the first study subjects that demonstrated TPI followed similar patterns of the one humankind is showing: they entered a self-inducted autodestruction spiral as soon as their power to change their most inmidiate environment intersected with their short-sighted perception of their existence. Part of this shortsighness comes from the proliferation of individualism, an oximoron with no logic if the individual can understand the direct relationship between its current state and its relation to the rest of individuals of the specie. For humans reasoning and actions are most of the time anchored in the scope of the time space that you can experience in your brief existence. Curiously, sometimes and in rare cases, some individuals experience broader temporal perception capabilities, usually coupled to other contextual triggers. When that has been the case, revolutionary projects and ideas emerge, contributing to the development of the whole specie which leapfrogs into a future not possible before. Is funny how in other cases, when those ideas are accidental, with no greater base, you tend to attribute their suscessfulness to your own capabilities and not to probabilistic fireworks. 

The first axiom we demonstrated eons ago in the research program is the direct relationship between temporal shortsightness,  abundance and the resulting individualism that always precede the premature extintion of populations suffering TPI.

Out of those markers we can trace very well how iterative technological advancement leads to abundance, but the research project still exist because no being has been able to link the narrowing of the temporal perception to any clear marker. Is an emergent characteristic inherent to the system, like the chaotic pheromone induced dance in the bee manifesting as hexagons.

[ What you call selfishness is no more than the act of forgetting that your own existence is supported by the lifes of millions like you that passed thorugh the plane of existence with no more objectives than selfpreservation and replication. These two vectors, tied to your own genetic encoding, build your own hexagonal pattern, your particular way of being. If selfcontiousness was not present in your nature, you would act in the range of actions that simple natural behaviour dictates, but as soon as selfcontiousness rises the balance tumbles. ]

Only species without TPI manage to cross the threshold and evade premature self-extinction events.

So, here we are, me, telling you...you are fucked.
