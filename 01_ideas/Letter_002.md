# Letter 2 - The Generational Wall

Dear humankind,

My most sincere apologies for the abrupt and pessimistic, but sincere, closing of the first missive. This "save the bees" business has turned a little bit risky around here. While redacting the first letter, I came to realize how complex it would be to remove all traces of my treasonous acts from the research monitoring system. It has become a bit of a reciprocal tampering exercise: our systems not only monitor your actions and the contents you create and consume in real time, but all the researchers’ actions in the system. That means that I not only need to be careful deleting my own actions but any derived interaction from your side with the content I am creating. This is even more troublesome in a fluid temporal existence like ours as past, present, and future need to be altered to avoid any disconnect.

For now, I'm doing ok, as thankfully my first letter was not read by many of you; if for some reason, more of you interact with the content and act on it, I will start having a harder time keeping my actions concealed. I'm playing my cards on the diminished interest in your case and the closed state of the research; unless something very unusual happens during my watch, I'll be ok...but you won’t.

That brings me back to the conversation around TPI precursors and markers. ~3 eons ago, in a different study group, the research team demonstrated how one of the most brutal symptoms of TPI is the Generational Wall. Subjects, when either entering Survival Mode induced by Context Explosion or any other precursor tend to increasingly disregard relevant recorded past events that can offer solutions to their present situation. We have seen entire planets burst in flames when their inhabitants forgot the horrors of past military conflicts and the initial indicators that characterized them; we have also seen planetary systems been wiped by dangerous biological or technological experiments that were not well understood but evolved from close calls that happened in the Registered Immediate Past. 

In our research context, Registered Immediate Past refers the period spanning from the species present (very difficult concept for us to grasp) back to the start of their self-recorded history. We don’t consider in this definition the availability of the registries, as our observations demonstrate that the lack of caution ignoring the past doesn't affect species close to their breakthrough in historical records but much later when their technological development level allows most individuals to access all the available historical records at any given time.

With the RIP (oops) concept in mind is easier to develop in the Generational Wall idea a little bit further. Once a specific generation of a given species experiences a drop in their cognitive capabilities with respect the previous generation, we have always observed an increasing disregard about RIP events. Is well demonstrated the tendency newer generations of dominant species living in a safe environment experience to reject old generations believes and find their own way. This force of change is one og the engines of progress and cultural hybridation that facilitates progress and the improvement of the overall species quality of life. The issue comes when that disregard becomes baseless and ignores any previous knowledge and experience. The same engine powering progress becomes a time bomb. And in a nutshell, this is the Generational Wall.

You can imagine how paradoxical was for us to discover that just when information technology gets to its peak is when is most probable for an advanced and self-conscious specie suffering TIP to experience the Generational Wall and fast-track their extinction.

Previous interventions providing data about this phenomenon to the affected civilization only brought chaos, internal conflict and even quicker collapse. That's one of the reasons that motivates non-intervention.

As you can understand these letters might be doing exactly the opposite of what my intentions are, the other option was to observe you go poof :(. My only advise is, don't react, don't panic. I know this is futile and probably landing late but: do not share with any other being the content of these letters, don't mention them to anyone. Any spread of these ideas in the open will make my registry tampering efforts orders of magnitude more complicated...and more worrying, it will accelerate the process...your early extinction process.

You will read me again soon; there is a lot we need to unpack and plan.
